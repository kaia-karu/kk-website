Hobbies and Interests

One of my hobbies is drawing. I have taken many art classes since I was young and have always had a passion for drawing using many different materials. The mediums which have been my favorite are: Charcoal, graphite, and marker. I really enjoy realism and find it to be such a rewarding practice that requires lots of patience.

Music

Another of my interests is music. I have a very musical family and have been surrounded by it constantly. I have been in a few different choirs throughout my life and I really enjoy singing. I have messed around with a few intstruments but my voice is the one that I use the most. Other than singing, I love to discover new music and enjoy collecting vinyl. There are a wide range of genres that I listen to but some artists of note are: The Beatles, Björk, Yung Lean, and Pierce the Veil.

Misc

Some of my other interests include, history, film, and videogames. Throughout my time at Loyola University, I have taken many classes surrounding Eastern European History in the 20th Century. This area of history, is very interesting and its great to learn about intellectuals of this time like Polish writer Czesław Miłosz. Regarding film, I am a fan of genres like, true crime and horror especially involving apocolypses and dystopias. I am also a fan of games like: Minecraft, Halo, and I am an avid Tetris player.
